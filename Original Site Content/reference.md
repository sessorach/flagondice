Bleed

At the start of your turn, if you have any Bleeds, remove one and take 1 damage.

Exhaustion

For each stack of

Exhaustion

you have, your maximum Health is reduced by 1.

Exhaustion goes away when you get a chance to rest for the night.

Some effects give you  "temporary" Exhaustion, which is tracked separately. These go away at the end of the encounter.

Immobilize

Being

Immobilized means you can’t move. You can still be Pushed by things, and you can still Shift.

Push

Push

ing someone

is like Shifting, except when you do it to an enemy. Unless otherwise

stated, you Push things in a direction directly away from you. If

someone is Pushed into an obstacle that isn’t a creature, they probably

just stop there (unless the GM decides something more specific happens).

Shift

Shifting

is like moving, except it doesn’t count against your Speed, and you can

Shift through spaces with enemies in them (but not end up in an

occupied space).
